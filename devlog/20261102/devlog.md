# JetLag now plays in your browser

JetLag 2021 was a Windows-only download. This page now carries the one JetLag: JetLag 2020 and JetLag 2023 combined, rebuilt in Odin and compiled to WebAssembly, so it runs right here on the page. The old Windows download is gone.

What you get:

- A scrolly-block endless runner. Your jet rides at the top of the screen while the board scrolls up past it, dragging a six-cell tail.
- Steer between the blocks, grab the coins, and bank them at a green $ before you crash. Crash with unbanked coins and they are lost.
- Every turn scores the triangular number of the ticks since your last turn, so a long straight run beats a nervous zig-zag.
- Sound effects, a mute key (M), and touch controls: tap the left or right half of the screen to turn.

Controls: Left / Right or A / D to turn, Z, Space or a tap to start.

The latest version always lives at https://playdeezgames.github.io/jetlag/ and the itch build can lag behind it.
